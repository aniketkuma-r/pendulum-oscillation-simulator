## Pendulum Oscillation Simulator built using Vite, React, Redux Toolkit, TypeScript and Mantine UI.

### It simulates the motion of a pendulum in two scenarios:

- Ideal Case: A theoretical scenario where the pendulum experiences no friction or air resistance, allowing it to swing indefinitely.
- Real Case with Damping: A more realistic scenario where the pendulum's motion is affected by a damping coefficient, simulating energy loss due to friction or air resistance, eventually bringing the pendulum to rest.
- 
### The project involves simulating the motion of a pendulum in React.js to visually demonstrate the principles of oscillatory motion, energy conservation, and damping due to air resistance. The simulation allows users to adjust parameters like pendulum length, mass, and gravity and observe their effects on the motion. Key features include:

1. Realistic Oscillation: Simulates pendulum motion using trigonometric equations for angle, velocity, and energy.


2. Energy Graphs: Displays potential, kinetic, and total energy variations over time.


3. Damping Effects: Accounts for air resistance, reducing swing amplitude over time using drag force calculations.


4. Interactive Controls: Adjustable sliders for string length, mass, and gravity, along with a play/pause button for better exploration.


5. Real-time Visualization: Shows forces like tension and weight acting on the pendulum and includes free-body diagrams.


The use of Vite allows for fast development and optimized builds, while React manages the user interface, providing real-time visualization of the pendulum's motion. TypeScript ensures type safety, improving code quality and reducing bugs. The simulation likely allows users to adjust parameters such as length, mass, gravity, and the damping coefficient to see how these affect the oscillation behavior in both the ideal and real cases.
